# 🎯 100 Days of Code: Master Project Curriculum

## Curriculum Overview
- **Progression:** 3 Difficulty Tiers (Easy → Medium → Hard).
- **Daily Structure:** Every day consists of 1 mini-project broken into 4 sequential tasks:
  1. **Task 1 (HTML):** Semantic structure & markup.
  2. **Task 2 (CSS):** Visual styling, layout & typography.
  3. **Task 3 (JavaScript):** Interactivity, DOM manipulation & logic.
  4. **Task 4 (Integration):** Final polish, edge cases & verification.
